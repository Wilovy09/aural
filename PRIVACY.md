# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 12, 2026_

Aural is an open-source YouTube Music client. It does not require an Aural account and Aural's developers do not operate any server that receives, stores or analyzes your data, music library, listening history or credentials.

## Data stored on your device

Aural keeps the following only on your device, in the app's private folder:

- your YouTube Music sign-in session, used to load your library and play music;
- your settings (text and interface size, animated covers, lyrics translation, Discord activity);
- the queue and position of the song playing, so playback can resume;
- the devices paired through Aural Connect;
- cached images and lookups, so they are not downloaded again.

Signing out removes the session. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

## Third-party services

To work, Aural connects directly from your device to these services. What they receive is limited to what each feature needs, and it is handled under their own privacy policies:

| Service | What it is used for | What it receives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google / YouTube Music | Sign-in, your library, search, playback and likes | Your session and your requests, as with any YouTube Music client |
| Apple Music catalog | Covers, animated covers and lyrics | The title, artist and album of the song |
| LRCLIB, Musixmatch, NetEase, KuGou and other lyrics providers | Synchronized lyrics and translations | The title, artist and duration of the song |
| Google Translate | Machine translation of lyrics, when no other translation exists | The lines of the lyrics being translated |
| Discord (desktop only) | Showing the song playing as your Discord activity | See below |

Aural does not send your Google credentials to anyone other than Google.

## Discord activity

On computers, if the Discord app is running, Aural tells it the song you are playing: its title, artist, album, cover image link, how far into the song you are, and a link to the song on YouTube Music. Discord shows this on your profile to other people, under Discord's own privacy policy.

This happens only between Aural and the Discord app on the same computer. Aural does not receive any information about your Discord account. It can be turned off in **Cuenta → Reproducción → Actividad en Discord** (Account → Playback → Discord activity), and it is cleared whenever playback pauses or stops.

## Aural Connect

Aural Connect lets you play on and control another Aural on your local network. Devices find each other on your Wi-Fi and pair once with a 4-digit code. What they exchange (what is playing, the queue and playback commands) stays on your local network and does not pass through any server.

## Analytics and logs

Aural does not include analytics, advertising or telemetry. Diagnostic logs are written only on your device and are never uploaded. You may share them yourself when reporting a problem.

## Children

Aural is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
